AQUORA 0.6.5 — FM-1 guide

Generative Sound Sanctuary · Sound • Water • Resonance

Turn the master down before installation. The original eight-second FM introduction follows the master. Deliberate key, button, encoder or master movement skips it; the skip input is consumed. Playback starts on PLAY. PLAY stops new notes and fades to digital silence; press again to resume. The eight roles are Lead, Bass, Pads, Harmony, Piano, Plucks, Choir and Drums.

Navigation and editing

ALGORITHM selects PLAY / SOUND / EVOLVE / MIX / DRUMS / SETUP / AIR. HOME returns to PLAY. SELECT chooses a role (or a channel on MIX). PRESET changes and auditions the selected patch; on DRUMS it auditions a kit. K1–K4 always show their current assignments and values.

SOUND: EDIT cycles directly through editable sections; there is no empty category picker. SELECT remains role selection. Instrument K1 selects patch, K2 brightness, K3 attack, K4 release. Motion applies to Lead/Bass; select one of those roles. Patch shape zero means the original patch shape.

Quick Find: hold HOME and tap EDIT, or SETUP → EDIT to Quick Find. K1 chooses a category or individual parameter; K2 opens its editable location and highlights the physical knob. Categories are shortcuts; the following parameter entries are the canonical directory. All routes share the same parameter IDs and setters. MIX owns channel level/pan/send/mute/solo; SOUND MIX SHORTCUT and PLAY level shortcuts edit those same values. Bus shortcuts edit the same Nature/frequency/binaural controls, without independent state.

EVOLVE: EDIT cycles Loop, New Song, Groove, Development, Music Mode. New Song and Reroll require STOP; turn the action knob to arm and press SEL to execute. Reroll keeps motif identity and locked sounds. Freeze holds phrase evolution. Development is Stable / Subtle / Flowing / Adventurous with selectable pace; Next requests a boundary evolution. The event graph shows observed events, not predicted notes. Returning motif memory runs every fourth evolution.

DRUMS: ARP or OCT+ opens drums. EDIT cycles Pattern, Sound/Mix, Mute/Solo. Pattern K1 enables, K2 density 0–8, K3 HIT 0–100%, K4 level. Density means placement; HIT means optional-hit probability; Level is volume; Presence means mix emphasis. None is a brightness knob. PRESET or kit K1 on Sound/Mix changes and auditions the kit. All nineteen IDs are preserved. Kick/hand transients and kit-dependent timbres are brighter in 0.6.2.

MIX: eight-track console; SELECT focuses a track or source bus. FX opens the large channel strip; FX returns to the overview. K1 level, K2 pan, K3 FX send, K4 solo. SEL toggles mute; LFO toggles solo. EDIT cycles All, Master FX, Reset, Active, Bus Shortcuts. Reset needs its arm and press SEL. Master FX includes reverb, delay, Auto Gain and Auto Level. Meters are real engine peaks. The channel strip shows level/peak/clip/pan/send. Meters are sampled peaks, not a calibrated loudness meter.

SOUND / Nature: choose a manual bed, level and audition. AUTO uses the intention/style song profile. Selecting Off locks it off; AUTO explicitly unlocks selection. Manual beds stay fixed across new songs and intention changes. Nature Character provides style, activity and distance. Level is independent of activity. Birds and Forest use bounded irregular procedural phrases, foliage and stereo depth; no field-recording authenticity claim. The four added beds are Florida Night, Twilight Meadow, Crystal Creek and Mountain Rain. Six appended FM patches and four FM kits preserve old IDs.

Frequency: reference tone is a separate precise layer. Frequency Lock places pitched roles at the chosen fixed reference, with optional textures. A4 432/440 is a separate tuning choice (ENV toggles it). Binaural produces independent left/right carriers at carrier ± half the difference, after shared FX. Headphones are required for the intended binaural presentation. Beat range is 0.5–40 Hz; carrier 80–1000 Hz. These are musical features without medical efficacy claims.

Keyboard: PLAY EDIT switches Play Along / Key Change. Perfect Key quantizes live notes to scale. Step and half-step queue notes to boundaries; short released taps cancel; STOP clears pending notes. Key Change commits at a loop boundary. Strength/feel and groove are separate from key quantization.

AIR: opt-in receive-only BLE-strength experiment, OFF by default. First tab K1 radio, K2 send target, K3 far calibration, K4 Hold. EDIT opens FX mapping: K1 Reverb Bloom / Echo Throw / Bloom + Echo, K2 depth, K3 manual modulation; target has its single home on the sensor section. EDIT returns to the sensor tab. FX choice/depth persist; radio, temporary output and Hold reset safely at boot. Existing manual FX amounts are preserved; disabling or signal loss returns modulation to zero. The shared FX bus is global; target chooses the role whose send is modulated. When packets are absent, use manual K3. A stored calibration does not mean live signal. Raw/filtered strength, packet rate and output are shown separately. This is one inferred RF dimension, not XY tracking. Physical reception and hand response still require the owner's FM-1 test. Advanced gesture presets are not included because real gesture reliability is not established.

SETUP: EDIT cycles Display/Lights, Banks, About, Quick Find. In the first tab rotate the SELECT encoder (or press SEL) to switch Lights and Appearance. Appearance K1 selects the panel skin (Stock/Aurora/Neon Lagoon/Solar Bloom/Midnight Prism), K2 Small/Medium/ Large AQUORA Sans, K3 Black/White background, K4 Original/Resonance Plate. Global preferences save automatically after 1.2 seconds while stopped. Display choices are independent of arrangement banks. Large values use full cells; bank confirmations occupy an upper row.

Lights On lights the fitted outputs, Keys lights all fitted keys with decorative buttons off, Off disables decorative lights, Dance follows actual musical events. LED Test steps matrix slots; physical LED mapping/brightness still needs owner verification.

SAVE: press the physical SAVE button once on any page, while playing or stopped, to store the complete current song in the active Bank 1–4 (default 1). The PLAY header shows B1–B4. An existing save is replaced without arming. “Saving Bank N” remains visible until CRC-protected flash commit and readback complete; only then does “Saved to Bank N” appear. If a write fails, retry SAVE. The preceding valid sector remains available after an interrupted write. Do not remove power until confirmation; a live flash save can take seconds. Repeated SAVE coalesces one further snapshot of the latest state.

OCT− opens Banks; K1 chooses the destination and press SEL explicitly loads the selected bank while stopped. SAVE always stores; it never loads, resets the mixer, creates a New Song or rerolls sounds. New Song/Reroll and Reset Mix use their labelled arm knob followed by SEL, while stopped as applicable. Bank fields preserve all eight roles/patches, melodic and returning motifs, intentions, tuning/reference/binaural, FX, mixer/mutes/solo, drums, Nature, locks, keyboard grid/strength/feel, loop length, motion and evolution pace. Appearance, lighting, AIR mapping and active bank cursor are global preferences, saved automatically when stopped; stop briefly before powering off to persist changes to those global preferences. Old bank/patch/kit IDs remain compatible.

Visuals: idle PLAY enters fullscreen after ten seconds. Deliberate panel interaction exits without applying that first edit; keys continue to play. Battery fades after about two seconds; normal PLAY restores full controls for both visualizers. Resonance Plate has thirteen smoothly changing artistic intention geometries, frequency/tuning form changes, actual role meters and onset envelopes. These are cymatics-inspired art, not scientific water models. Only visible graphs capture samples or generate geometry. Hidden graphs do not stop musical evolution, effects or AIR control.

Performance: GLO cycles diagnostics. Mean audio/FX, callback p95/p99/max, DMA XRUN delta, voices, graphics time and blit rate are separate quantities. REC forces graphics to single core until reboot. Host timing is a software proxy and cannot establish delivered LCD FPS or FM-1 XRUN performance.

Owner hardware acceptance

Compare first playback immediately after updating with a complete power-off/ power-on at identical stock settings. Capture callback mean/p95/p99/max and XRUN deltas with binaural OFF / ON / OFF, then dense drums/Nature/FX and each visualizer. Compare the 0.6.1 cold-boot baseline (83% Audio+FX in the supplied window), separately from its first-install 97% / 82-XRUN case. No measured percentage improvement on physical hardware is claimed by software tests.

Check each fitted LED, battery state, hand-shadow calibration/dropout, all nineteen drum kits on speaker/headphones, Nature realism, font legibility, fullscreen return, bank recall, direct updater and one-hour maximum-feature soak. Delivered 20 FPS, RF behavior, first-install state and audio quality remain owner verification.

MUSIC MODE and automatic motion (0.6.5)

EVOLVE → EDIT four times → MUSIC MODE: K1 Standard / Cinematic / Neoclassical. Standard retains balanced ambient grammar. Cinematic adds long sustained harmony, spacious bass and sparse pulses; Neoclassical uses piano arpeggios, plucks and countermelody. Each intention/mode change makes a fresh unlocked realization. Patch/FX locks, manual Nature/OFF, mixer mutes and Freeze still take precedence. K2 LEAD MOTION and K3 BASS MOTION select AUTO (turn left) or MANUAL (right). Editing a role's SOUND → MOTION glide/vibrato switches that role to MANUAL. AUTO reapplies intention × mode defaults. Full banks recall exact saved sounds, mode and overrides. Old banks load Standard and preserve saved custom motion. All controls are also available in Quick Find.

Resonance Plate replaces the secondary visualizer with a 25×25 standing-wave field and interpolated nodal contours over a circular medium. Intention and reference frequency shape the field; actual role energy and onsets excite it, and silence lets it decay. It is artistic cymatics, not a scientific simulator. It does not appear in the static branded startup. Original remains available.

v0.6.5 workflow and safe SAVE

ALGORITHM chooses the main page; EDIT advances the numbered section. Turning SELECT chooses a role/item; pressing SEL performs the action shown on screen. HOME returns to PLAY. MIX overview K1/FX opens the channel strip. SEL mutes, LFO or strip K4 solos. MASTER FX is EDIT2/5: K1 Reverb, K2 Delay, K3 Auto Gain, K4 Auto Level. Auto Gain gently balances input peaks; Auto Level applies ensemble balance and output ceiling. These are distinct controls, not extra sound tracks. Bus shortcuts now open the canonical sound/FX home with K1, rather than duplicate editable settings. The existing eight musical tracks and five source buses remain.

EVOLVE Loop owns tempo, Freeze and16/32/64 steps. Development owns variation, evolution, pace and next phrase. Groove owns style/strength. Empty cells do nothing. New Song/Reroll require STOP: turn K1/K2 to arm, then SEL confirms once. SAVE only stores the active bank; it never confirms a song/reset action. PLAY/STOP, EDIT, HOME or changing main page cancels armed actions. Reroll retains motif and locks.

SAVE uses a bounded command fence and captures the selected destination before writing. Optional geometry yields to save progress. The inactive flash sector is committed with CRC/readback; only then does Saved appear. A five-second watchdog reports failure without falsely declaring success; any in-flight flash operation is safely drained before retry. Fullscreen defers while saving; SAVE wakes a fullscreen display. SETUP/About shows snapshot/flash/total times and truthful state. Rotate SELECT on About for per-phase flash elapsed times (erase/payload/header/CRC verification), request/retry counts, worker-busy and suspend counts. These are main-loop latency diagnostics, not extra audio work.

Six appended FM bass patches (IDs30–35): Pure Sub FM, Rounded Ember FM, Wood Resonance FM, Harmonic Root FM, Plucked Earth FM, Cinema Depth FM. Old IDs and banks remain valid. Curated mode/intention pools use the new unlocked basses; manual locks remain authoritative. There are36 patches, eight tracks,16 voices.

Startup is an independent rotating/drawing, palette-colored spiral over a bright theme field, with a stable AQUORA title/version and preserved soft FM chime. Neither regular visualizer runs during intro. Every deliberate control skips it. White panels are vivid; textual ink remains dark. AQUORA Sans is required at compile time and its atlas fingerprint is visible in About. A small precomputed italic face appears only in expressive Music Mode descriptions; navigation, numeric readings and safety/confirmation text remain upright and high contrast.

Performance cadence targets20FPS when headroom permits, reduces optional refresh under pressure, and recovers without a permanent freeze. Grid17×17, separable standing modes, cap256 contours, band-clipped lines and reduced Original geometry bound fallback cost. Host software acceptance cannot prove actual FM-1 FPS, callback deadlines/XRUNs, RF response, flash latency or listening quality.
