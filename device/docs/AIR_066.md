# AIR: measured one-dimensional radio modulation

AIR is receive-only ambient Bluetooth signal-strength modulation. It needs a
nearby broadcaster; it cannot promise hand tracking without real packets and
it does not measure an XY position. The screen distinguishes radio off,
starting, RF error, listening/no broadcaster, received packets, far/near
calibration, active signal and signal loss. Raw/smoothed strength and packet
counts are measured, not a decorative scan animation.

On AIR's sensor section, turn K3 right with your hand away to record FAR.
Move near the antenna, wait for the strength to settle, then turn right again
to record NEAR. Either attenuation or enhancement is accepted when the measured
change is sufficient. Turn K3 left to reset. A completed calibration remains
locked until reset; absent/stale samples cannot calibrate. Signal loss returns
modulation smoothly to baseline. Physical sensitivity is owner-unverified.

EDIT opens Reverb / Echo / combined FX, depth and a manual fallback control.
AIR % is the smoothed modulation signal; REV/ECHO readouts show actual DSP wet
values. FX FOCUS biases the selected role's send into the **shared** effect bus;
it is not an isolated per-track reverb processor. All-tracks focus controls the
common blend. No additional FX instance or audio buffer is allocated.

BLE performance MIDI is deferred and has no active owner in this release.
USB MIDI and AIR may operate together; neither USB configuration nor incoming
performance messages start or stop the radio.
