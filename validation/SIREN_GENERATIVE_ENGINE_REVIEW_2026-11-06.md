## What is demonstrably useful
1. **A higher-level composition form above individual note generation.** SIREN's `sb_form` assembles style-weighted intro/groove/break/build/drop/peak/outro sections with varying lengths; `sb_step16` varies which layers generate events based on section/energy. For AQUORA, use *original*, non-rave intention/style-driven movement (arrival, gentle unfold, bloom, drifting, hush, release, motif return); do not copy SIREN's musical style, section probabilities or source.
2. **Multiple timescales.** SIREN distinguishes track-level form from periodic motif mutation (`sb_mutate`, each eight bars) and individual step-level events. AQUORA already mutates/restores motifs at phrase boundaries, but role participation needs an explicit upper layer. Preserve returning motif, long evolution, musical continuity and user-initiated evolution.
3. **Reproducible but fresh variation.** SIREN uses a seed and separated hashed PRNG draws for form, sounds, motifs and step events. AQUORA already has seeded `song_draw` and distinct RNG fields. Use its existing machinery to independently plan phrases/roles without changing audio-random consumption or making a new song irreproducible.
4. **Intentional contrast and instrument eligibility.** SIREN gates arps, hooks, pads, stabs, percussion, etc. based on song section and energy; sound palettes are style-specific and can keep coherent keys/progressions. AQUORA should derive *role opportunities* from intention + music mode + phrase/harmony + energy + evolution, not from a fixed always-on role set.
5. **Deterministic auditing and honest quality evidence.** SIREN maintains C-vs-prototype event parity and host audio/performance tests. Extend AQUORA's existing role-event tests with actual post-gain per-role stem/meter evidence and active-set timelines. Event count > 0 does NOT prove audible contribution.

## Specific AQUORA gap observed in current source
- `device/dsp/engine.c::trigger` increments `phrase`, updates motifs, applies a five-position `section=(phrase/4)%5` energy contour and triggers bass/pad/piano/lead/harmony/guitar/choir/drums on fixed patterns with occasional probability/section gates. This mostly changes notes and density, not independently planned entrances/exits.
- `tests/arrangement_roles_060.c` asserts `engine.tracks[role].events` for all roles across high-variation simulations; it **does not** demonstrate dynamic role mixtures, audible post-gain stems, sustained crossfades, or correct real-world mixer metering.
- Owner observes the same ~4–5 audible parts remain for the whole song while the others never play. This observation needs a targeted diagnosis; the exact fault (scheduler, patch, voice stealing, mixer routing/gain or meter display) is unverified.
- **Important SIREN limitation:** `sb_new_track` chooses `layer_on` flags once for a whole new track; `sb_step16` has section-aware musical gates. SIREN therefore does not itself prove gradual track-to-track gain fading or that all optional layers rotate during one track. Design those behaviors independently for AQUORA.

## Original AQUORA implementation proposal (do not execute until authorized sole build owner acquires lock)
1. **Lightweight arrangement plan / role-intention matrix:** At phrase/section boundaries, compute a small deterministic plan (section kind, desired energy, harmonic anchor, roles eligible, desired level per role, optional lead handoff) using AQUORA's 13 intentions, 3 music modes, 16/32/64 step loops, Freeze/evolution policy and current generator seed. Variable durations; avoid rigid recurring sequences, new UI bloat and arbitrary minimum/maximum active-track quotas. All eight roles must work and be available, but zero obligation for each to appear in every song or at once.
2. **Real role entrances/exits:** Optionally leave some parts silent, let others enter/recede when musically justified, and periodically rediscover voices during suitable evolution. Respect locks, manual mute/solo, style, intention, chord progression and energy. A sparse piece can stay sparse; an expansive one may naturally use all eight.
3. **Click-free transitions:** Target per-role gain envelope or controlled event gating with short smooth fades on phrase boundaries; avoid cutting long notes or reverb tails; instrument-specific fade and attack times. Precompute orchestration outside inner per-sample DSP; no heap allocation, flash writes or UI work in audio interrupt.
4. **Maintain identity:** Preserve existing motif-home return, intention Solfeggio/reference tone exactness, binaural channel separation, key/scale coherence, natural beds, drums, user-controlled New Song/Reroll and extended-session evolution. Honor Freeze (no spontaneous role movement while frozen), true user mutes/locks and save-bank recall/migration requirements.
5. **Performance and diagnostics:** Separately log (a) eligible/assigned (b) generated notes (c) accepted voices (d) post-mixer gain/RMS/peaks (e) UI meter states per role; inspect why any roles remain silent. Prevent excessive simultaneity/voice stealing and cap CPU/XRUN against no-lag v0.6.5. Preserve stock visualizer and #17 visualizer work.
6. **Acceptance:** Test deterministic multi-hour equivalent traces across 13 intentions × 3 modes × 16/32/64 steps and multiple seeds, plus quiet/expansive contrasts; verify varied active-role combinations **when musically warranted**, not artificial quotas. Test crossfade discontinuities and audible stem changes; Freeze, solo/mute, manual locks, saved banks, STOP/PLAY; compare host callback time/XRUN/peak to v0.6.5. Owner physical listening and MIXER verification stays UNVERIFIED until device test.
