# AQUORA v0.7.14 development checkpoint

Starts at the published v0.7.13 tag a46e113ffdccd6c04bfade632c1d76d263628f85.

This checkpoint adds five-mode tempo planning, style-driven retiming, optional-drum HIT gating, a recorded-vocal lane enabled on fresh preferences, KIRA-9's actual portrait renderer, vocal signal view, stronger space effects, and a live slicer display. The portrait renderer and pose layout are from KIRA-9 7e4258c2f94da832a7bad3395d7a364b78c2e157 (MIT license in device/kira_face).

Remaining release gates include independent vocal pitch correction and FX, broader mixer processors, full drum-lane programming/audition and kit differentiation, transport and bank regression evidence, native and browser packaging, release and deployment. Physical performance and artistic acceptance are pending.
