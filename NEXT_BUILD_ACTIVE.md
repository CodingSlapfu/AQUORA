To satisfy literal “only that frequency” rather than just equal-note fundamentals, provide **strict single-sine signal path for pitched tracks** (avoid timbre harmonics, modulation sidebands, chorus/detuning and out-of-band layered pitch components while locked); volume envelopes and FX can add spectral transients, so do not claim mathematically perfect single-line acoustic output through the full processor without measurement.
