## P1 — Nature Beds present but not audibly distinguishable on FM-1 (new hardware feedback 2026-11-04)

- [ ] **Investigate on-device audibility and quality of Nature Beds.** Owner can find the feature in AQUORA 0.3.0 but reports hearing **no discernible nature sounds** in normal music playback. Source has eight K1 choices (`Off, Wind, Water, Rain, Forest, Ocean, Birds, Leaves`) and K2 nature level 0–100%. Nature defaults to **18%** in `sr_init`, then `render_samples()` uses `bed()*bed_level*transport_env` and adds only **half** to each L/R channel. `bed()` is low-level synthesized noise/tones, some with very low gain; Birds calls are intentionally sporadic. Additionally `if(e->solo<SR_TRACKS)nature=0` silences all Nature Beds during *any* track solo; scene changes overwrite `bed_type` via `sr_set_scene`. These are confirmed code behaviors, **not yet a proven cause** of the owner's observation. Do not mark as no signal without verifying a 100% Nature level / no-solo render.

Improve dynamics and distinct identity without expensive audio sample playback/decoding or new DSP bottlenecks. Birds should feature sufficiently recurring recognizable chirps while retaining ambient spaciousness; Ocean/Water/Rain should feel like their labels, not just near-silent generic noise. Preserve nature Off and master-zero/STOP silence.
