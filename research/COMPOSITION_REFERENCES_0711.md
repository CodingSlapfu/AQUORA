# AQUORA v0.7.11 composition references

These tracks supersede the creative references used for earlier versions. Intention frequencies and tuning are separate from these musical references. The table maps published descriptions and catalogues to original score rules. Several individual tracks have catalogue evidence and album-level descriptions only. The rules are engineering interpretations, and no track was transcribed or sampled.

| References | What to study | Original score treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aphex Twin — Xtal, Tha, Heliosphan, Ageispolis | How a persistent pulse gives atmospheric harmony and economical motifs room to unfold. Compare the quality of the grounded kick and bass in the quieter passages with the melodic lift. | Share harmonic landmarks across the tracks. Keep kick-aligned bass movement and hold longer chords through quieter Standard sections. |
| Plaid — Eyen, Ralome, Do Matter, Hawkmoth | How a short instrumental figure leaves space for a second line, with cadence and phrasing shaping the relationship between them. Compare understated guitar and keyboard placement in Eyen with the warmer harmonic color of Hawkmoth. | Develop the actual theme rhythm, offset an answering onset only into a free slot, and use common tones in accompaniment across section boundaries. |
| µ-Ziq — Hasty Boom Alert, Brace Yourself Jason, My Little Beautiful | Contrast animated breakbeats with a clear melodic foreground. Listen for the release that follows dense rhythmic passages. | Preserve the theme head while varying its answering tail in Experimental Ambient. Assign broken groove, a separate crest and a recognizable reprise explicit places in the form. |
| Wagon Christ — Shadows, Saddic Gladdic, Musipal | How percussion, bass articulation and short harmonic replies make a pocket. Compare the tighter percussion and piano interplay of Saddic Gladdic with the wider palette of Musipal. | Retain the actual drum-pattern anchor for the bass. Pull back secondary roles through a suspension and use a shared cadence to bring featured parts forward. |
| Boards of Canada — Roygbiv, Olson, Dayvan Cowboy, Peacock Tail | How sustained tone color, length of phrase and gradual orchestral changes carry emotional progression. Compare Roygbiv's broad melodic span with the quieter transitions of the longer pieces. | Give Cinematic a statement, a choir-led departure, a bloom, and a return. Bring back the home material with new supporting voices and use an independent song-scale clock. |

## Additions for the recorded-vocal build

- µ-Ziq's “Goodbye, Goodbye” from *Royal Astronomy*, with vocals by Kazumi, is a reference for how a human voice can carry an emotional arrival inside an electronic arrangement. AQUORA uses other singers' cleared wordless vowel recordings, fitted to original chord tones and answered in alternating phrases. [Album](https://music.apple.com/us/album/royal-astronomy/723866487) · [Credits](https://musicbrainz.org/release/39902da6-ddcd-3598-889e-cf6da777274f)
- Squarepusher's *Hard Normal Daddy* is the separate rhythmic reference. Its breakbeat vocabulary informs the use of denser activity around a stable bass anchor in Experimental Ambient. [Squarepusher's release catalogue](https://squarepusher.net/release/228378-squarepusher-hard-normal-daddy?lang=en_US)
- “Brace Yourself Jason,” “Hasty Boom Alert” and “My Little Beautiful” appear on µ-Ziq's *Lunatic Harness*. [Planet Mu release catalogue](https://planet.mu/releases/lunatic-harness-25th-anniversary-edition/)
