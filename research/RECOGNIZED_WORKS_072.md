# AQUORA 0.7.2: recognized-work composition reference

Selection uses platform Top Songs / Essentials and established label catalogue entries, rather than arbitrary clips. No recording was generated, downloaded, sampled, transcribed or copied. This is documented compositional research and original engineering interpretation; it is not a claim of a new listening session or of artist imitation.

| Artist | Recognized reference works | Broad compositional lesson and AQUORA application |
|---|---|---|
| Aphex Twin | Xtal / Selected Ambient Works 85–92; Avril 14th | A stable pulse can support a memorable, economical melody; piano expressiveness needs space. Preserve motif returns and kick/bass pocket; give Neoclassical a two-bar question/answer with a cadence breath and articulated longer downbeat notes. |
| Plaid | Eyen; New Family / Double Figure | Complementary short instrumental figures and interlocking melodic voices can remain emotionally clear. Retain original three/five-step counterpoint, use a stable harmonic destination and phrase-shaped velocity rather than unrelated random notes. |
| µ-Ziq | Brace Yourself Jason; Hasty Boom Alert / Lunatic Harness | Ethereal melody and intricate rhythm need not become abrasive. Keep Experimental Ambient's original quiet interlocks and subtle melodic dynamics, stable bass/kick anchors and sparse transitions. |
| Wagon Christ | Saddic Gladdic; Shadows; Bend Over / Musipal | A supple downtempo pocket, bass articulation and contrasting textures hold a piece together. Retain original synthesized percussion, explicit mode+intention kit weights and coherent motif-preserving sound rerolls. No samples. |
| Boards of Canada | Roygbiv; Olson / Music Has the Right to Children; Dayvan Cowboy | A concise tonal identity, warm sustained space and a slow changing orchestration can carry a long arc. Retain harmonic voice-leading and motif homecoming; keep Cinematic's sustained lines gentler with longer phrasing, choir/strings and spacious FX. |

These lessons are shared across four distinct original modes: Standard uplifting melodic ambient electronica; Cinematic heavenly orchestral/choir ambient; Neoclassical expressive piano/harp/strings; Experimental Ambient intricate ethereal melodic IDM. They are aesthetic design goals; numerical tests cannot establish that music sounds heavenly or matches a listener's taste.
